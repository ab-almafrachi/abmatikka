Privacy Policy

Updated: 22 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AbMatikka processes personal data in connection with the website, enquiries, booking requests and tutoring activities. Personal data is processed in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679, other applicable data protection legislation and other legislation applicable to the business.

The aim is to collect and use personal data only to the extent necessary for AbMatikka's activities. Personal data is not sold or used for tracking users or for advertising profiling.

2. What Personal Data May Be Processed?

The personal data processed depends on how a person uses AbMatikka's services. Not all of the information listed below is collected from everyone.

Enquiries and Booking Requests

The following information may, for example, be processed through an enquiry or booking form:

  • name
  • email address
  • telephone number
  • student's grade level
  • preferred tutoring time and format
  • content of the enquiry or booking request
  • information relating to tutoring needs that the customer provides voluntarily
  • other information provided voluntarily.

During the Customer Relationship and Tutoring

During the customer relationship, information necessary for arranging tutoring may also be processed, such as:

  • agreed tutoring sessions and their times
  • practical information relating to the provision of tutoring
  • billing and payment information
  • information relating to customer communications with the customer
  • notes and information necessary for planning the tutoring.

As a general rule, AbMatikka does not request special categories of personal data, such as health data. Customers should not provide such information through a form or message unless providing it is exceptionally necessary for handling the matter. If such information is provided voluntarily, it will only be processed to the extent permitted by law and necessary for handling the matter.

3. Where Is Personal Data Obtained From?

Personal data is primarily obtained directly from the person concerned, for example through an enquiry or booking form, email, telephone call or other customer communication.

During the customer relationship, information may also arise from communication between AbMatikka and the customer, the arrangement of tutoring and billing.

Billing-related information may also be obtained through the billing service to the extent necessary to carry out billing.

4. How Is Personal Data Used?

Personal data is used only for specified and appropriate purposes. These purposes include, for example:

  • responding to enquiries
  • processing booking requests
  • agreeing on tutoring times and formats
  • planning and providing tutoring
  • managing the customer relationship
  • customer communication and practical arrangements
  • billing and payment administration
  • fulfilling accounting and other statutory obligations
  • ensuring service security and technical functionality
  • analysing website usage based on the user's consent.

Personal data is not used for purposes that conflict with this Privacy Policy or applicable data protection legislation.

5. Legal Bases for Processing Personal Data

Depending on the purpose of the processing, the processing of personal data is based on one or more of the following legal bases under the GDPR:

  • Contract or pre-contractual measures (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): for example, processing a booking request, arranging tutoring and managing the customer relationship where processing is necessary to enter into or perform a contract.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR): for example, fulfilling statutory obligations relating to accounting, taxation and billing.
  • Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): for example, maintaining the security of the service, information systems and website, preventing misuse and carrying out necessary customer communication where AbMatikka's legitimate interest is not overridden by the individual's rights.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR): for example, optional website analytics where the processing is based on consent.

Different processing activities may have different legal bases. Processing based on consent can be withdrawn at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

The AbMatikka website may use essential technical solutions that are necessary for the operation and security of the website and the proper functioning of forms.

In addition, the website may use Google Analytics 4 to statistically analyse website usage. Analytics is used only on the basis of the user's valid consent where applicable legislation requires consent.

Analytics may involve processing information such as browser and device information, website usage data and technical identifiers. Google states in its own privacy policy that personal data may also be processed outside the user's country of residence.

Consent to analytics can be given or refused through the cookie banner. If consent is not given, optional analytics will not be enabled. If cookie settings are available on the website, the user can also change their choice later through those settings.

7. Forms

AbMatikka's enquiry and booking forms may use the Tally service. Tally acts as a processor on behalf of AbMatikka to the extent that it processes form responses on AbMatikka's instructions.

According to Tally's statement, form data is encrypted in transit and at rest and stored in Europe. According to Tally, the sender of a form is responsible as the controller for processing the response data they collect, while Tally acts as the processor.

Information provided through a form is processed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

8. Data Processors and Service Providers

AbMatikka uses external service providers in its operations. Service providers may process personal data on behalf of AbMatikka where this is necessary to provide the service.

Examples of such service providers include:

  • Cloudflare – website operation, security, traffic delivery and protection
  • Tally – enquiry and booking forms
  • Google – email services and optional website analytics
  • UKKO.fi – billing and related services.

Service providers may process personal data only to the extent necessary to provide their services and permitted by applicable data protection legislation.

The use of service providers may involve independent processing activities for which the service providers are responsible in accordance with their own privacy policies.

9. Transfers of Personal Data Outside the EU or EEA

Some services used by AbMatikka may use data centres or service providers operating globally. As a result, personal data may in certain circumstances be transferred to or processed outside the EU or EEA.

If personal data is transferred outside the EU or EEA, the transfer is carried out as required by applicable data protection legislation and based on an appropriate transfer mechanism, such as an adequacy decision by the European Commission or, where necessary, appropriate safeguards.

AbMatikka aims to use services and settings that limit international transfers of personal data to what is necessary for the service.

10. To Whom May Personal Data Be Disclosed?

AbMatikka does not sell personal data or disclose it to external parties for marketing purposes.

Personal data may be processed or disclosed only where there is an appropriate legal basis, for example:

  • providing the service requires the use of an external processor
  • disclosure is required by law
  • disclosure is necessary to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim
  • there is another legal basis for disclosure under applicable data protection legislation.

Data is not disclosed to a wider group than is necessary for the relevant purpose.

11. Personal Data Concerning Minors

AbMatikka's services may be used to provide tutoring to minor students. Personal data concerning minors is processed with the same level of care as the personal data of other customers.

Arranging tutoring and communication concerning a minor may in practice be handled with the minor's guardian. However, a minor's possible right to use the service, enter into a contract or provide specific consent is always determined by applicable legislation.

AbMatikka aims to collect only information necessary for the tutoring and customer relationship concerning a minor and does not request unnecessary personal information.

12. Data Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as there is a purpose or other lawful basis for processing it.

The retention period depends on the purpose for which the data is used. There is therefore no single general retention period for all personal data.

Enquiries and Booking Information

Information relating to an individual enquiry or booking request is generally retained only for as long as necessary to process the enquiry or booking request, potentially establish a customer relationship and properly handle matters related to it.

If an enquiry does not result in a customer relationship and there is no other basis for retaining the information, the information is deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed for that purpose.

Customer Relationship Information

Information relating to the customer relationship may be retained throughout the customer relationship and thereafter to the extent necessary, for example, to fulfil statutory obligations, accounting and taxation requirements, contractual claims or legal protection.

Information Subject to Statutory Retention Requirements

Some information is subject to statutory retention periods. This may include, for example, accounting and tax-related information. Such information is not deleted until the applicable statutory retention period has ended, even if the customer relationship has already ended.

When personal data is no longer needed and there is no lawful basis for retaining it, it is appropriately deleted or anonymised.

13. Profiling and Automated Decision-Making

AbMatikka does not use personal data for automated decision-making that produces legal effects concerning an individual or similarly significantly affects an individual.

AbMatikka does not create marketing profiles of customers or sell personal data for profiling purposes.

14. Protection of Personal Data

AbMatikka uses appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, taking into account the nature of the data and the risks associated with its processing.

The website uses HTTPS encryption. Access to customer information is restricted to persons and services that have a justified need to access the information for processing purposes.

The security and data protection measures of service providers are also taken into account when selecting services.

No electronic service or data transfer is completely risk-free. AbMatikka takes appropriate measures to prevent unauthorised access, loss of data, misuse and other unlawful processing.

15. Rights of Data Subjects

Under applicable data protection legislation, data subjects have the right to exercise rights relating to the processing of their personal data. The application of these rights depends, among other things, on the legal basis for processing and the circumstances.

A data subject may have the right to:

  • obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning them is being processed
  • access their personal data
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • request deletion of personal data where permitted by law
  • request restriction of the processing of personal data where permitted by law
  • object to processing where the applicable law provides for such a right
  • receive personal data they have provided and transmit it to another system where the conditions required by law are met
  • withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

Not all rights are absolute. For example, information that must be retained by law cannot be deleted solely on the basis of a deletion request if a lawful retention obligation requires it to be retained.

Exercising Your Rights

A request concerning data subject rights can be sent by email:

tietosuoja@abmatikka.fi

The request should specify which right it concerns and, where applicable, which information it concerns. AbMatikka may request additional information to verify the requester's identity before providing information.

Data subject requests are handled within the time limits set by applicable data protection legislation.

16. Right to Lodge a Complaint with a Supervisory Authority

If a data subject believes that personal data has been processed in violation of data protection legislation, they have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

In Finland, the competent supervisory authority is:

Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto
https://tietosuoja.fi

A data subject has the right to use this legal remedy regardless of whether they have first contacted AbMatikka. However, contacting AbMatikka is generally the quickest way to resolve a possible issue directly.

17. Voluntary Provision of Personal Data

Providing personal data is generally voluntary. However, providing certain information may be necessary for AbMatikka to respond to an enquiry, arrange tutoring, provide the service or handle billing.

If a person does not provide information that is necessary to provide the service, AbMatikka may not be able to provide the relevant service or process the relevant request.

18. Accuracy of Information

Customers should, where possible, ensure that the contact information they provide to AbMatikka is accurate and up to date.

Data subjects have the right to request correction of inaccurate information as described in section 15 of this Privacy Policy.

19. Personal Data Breaches

If AbMatikka becomes aware of a personal data breach, AbMatikka will assess the situation and take the measures required by applicable data protection legislation.

If a personal data breach creates a risk to the rights and freedoms of a data subject such that notification is required by law, the competent supervisory authority and, where necessary, the affected individuals will be notified in accordance with applicable legislation.

AbMatikka is not responsible for the independent processing of personal data by third parties in their own services.
