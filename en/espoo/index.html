Math Tutoring in Espoo

Individualized teaching based on the student’s needs

AbMatikka provides math tutoring in Espoo for students in grades 1–9. Each lesson is planned according to the student’s starting level, goals and learning needs.

The goal is not only to solve individual exercises, but to help the student understand mathematics so that their skills continue to support them in future courses and exams.

Who is the tutoring suitable for?

The tutoring is suitable for students in Espoo who need support with mathematical fundamentals, homework, exam preparation or challenging topics.

It is equally suitable for students who need additional support and for students who want to strengthen their skills and improve their grades.

What happens during the lessons?

Each lesson begins with an assessment of the student’s starting level. Difficult topics are then explained step by step using clear examples, followed by guided practice until the student can solve problems independently.

  • Assessment of the starting level
  • Identifying areas that need improvement
  • Clear, step-by-step teaching
  • Guided practice
  • Building independent skills

Tutoring in Espoo and online

In-person tutoring is available in Espoo by arrangement. Tutoring can also be provided online anywhere in Finland, so location is not a limitation.

Tutoring is available in Finnish and English.

Free introductory lesson

The first 30-minute introductory lesson is completely free and does not commit you to further lessons.

During the introductory lesson, the student’s current skills are assessed, goals are discussed and possible future tutoring is planned.
