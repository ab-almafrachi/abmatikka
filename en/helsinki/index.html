Math Tutoring in Helsinki

Individualized teaching based on the student’s needs

AbMatikka provides math tutoring in Helsinki for students in grades 1–9. Each lesson is planned around the student’s current level, goals, and learning style.

The goal is not simply to solve exercises, but to help the student understand mathematics so that their skills remain strong in future courses, exams, and further studies.

Who is the tutoring suitable for?

Tutoring is suitable for school students in Helsinki who need support with math fundamentals, homework, exam preparation, or understanding challenging topics.

It is suitable both for students who need additional support and for those who want to strengthen their skills, develop problem-solving abilities, and aim for better grades.

What happens during the lessons?

Each lesson begins by assessing the student’s current skills. Difficult topics are then explained step by step using clear examples, followed by guided practice and a final check that the student can solve the exercises independently.

  • Assessment of current skills
  • Identifying difficult areas
  • Clear step-by-step teaching
  • Guided practice
  • Checking independent understanding

In-person tutoring in Helsinki and online

In-person tutoring is available in Helsinki by arrangement. Lessons can be organized flexibly according to the student’s needs, and online tutoring is also available throughout Finland.

Online tutoring provides the same individualized guidance as in-person lessons, so location does not prevent effective math learning.

Tutoring is available in both Finnish and English.

Free introductory lesson

The first 30-minute introductory lesson is completely free and does not commit you to further lessons.

During the introductory lesson, the student’s current skills are assessed, goals are discussed, and a suitable approach for future lessons is planned.

Why choose AbMatikka in Helsinki?

AbMatikka provides clear, calm, and individualized math tutoring, allowing students to progress at their own pace. The goal is to build lasting understanding of mathematics rather than simply get through an individual exam.

Tutoring is suitable for math homework help, exam preparation, reviewing difficult topics, and long-term development of mathematical skills.
