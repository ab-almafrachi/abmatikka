About tutoring

AbMatikka

AbMatikka provides clear and individualized math tutoring for students in grades 1–9.

I am a high school student and private math tutor. I provide math tutoring for students in grades 1–9. Teaching is based on clear, step-by-step explanations and the individual needs of each student.

I offer math tutoring in Espoo, Helsinki, Vantaa and online throughout Finland.

Teaching method

Teaching follows a structured process:

  • Assessment of the starting level
  • Identifying areas that need improvement
  • Clear, step-by-step teaching
  • Independent problem-solving skills

Additional information

Teaching is available in Finnish and English. Available in Espoo, Helsinki, Vantaa and online. Tutoring is also suitable for homework help and exam preparation.

Why choose this tutoring

Clear and individualized teaching

Every lesson is adapted to the student’s level and goals. Teaching progresses calmly and step by step so that understanding develops properly.

A clear structure in every lesson

Every lesson includes a starting-level check, guided practice and independent practice to ensure consistent learning.

Clear and transparent approach

Pricing, teaching methods and the booking process are fully transparent, with no hidden fees.

A risk-free first lesson

The first 30-minute introductory lesson is free and does not commit you to further lessons. During the lesson, the student’s level and needs are assessed.

Flexible availability

Teaching is available in Finnish and English, both online and in person Espoo, Helsinki and Vantaa.
