Online Math Tutoring via Google Meet

Individualized teaching based on the student’s needs

AbMatikka provides online math tutoring via Google Meet for students in grades 1–9 throughout Finland. Each lesson is planned around the student’s current level, goals, and learning style.

The goal is not simply to solve exercises, but to help the student understand mathematics so that their skills remain strong in future courses, exams, and further studies.

Who is online math tutoring suitable for?

Online tutoring is suitable for students throughout Finland who need support with math fundamentals, homework, exam preparation, or understanding challenging topics.

It is suitable both for students who need additional support and for those who want to strengthen their skills, develop problem-solving abilities, and aim for better grades.

How does Google Meet tutoring work?

Lessons are held through Google Meet, making it easy to participate using a computer or tablet. Before the lesson, the student receives a link that allows them to join with one click.

During lessons, exercises can be reviewed in real time, screens can be shared, the camera can be used, and the student’s own exercises can be reviewed just as in an in-person lesson. The tutor guides the student step by step throughout the lesson.

What happens during the lessons?

Each lesson begins by assessing the student’s current skills. Difficult topics are then explained step by step using clear examples, followed by guided practice and a final check that the student can solve the exercises independently.

  • Assessment of current skills
  • Identifying difficult areas
  • Clear step-by-step teaching
  • Guided practice
  • Checking independent understanding

Why does online tutoring work well?

Online Math Tutoring provides the same individualized guidance as in-person lessons, while allowing the student to participate from home. This saves travel time and makes flexible lesson times possible.

Because the lessons take place through Google Meet, students can receive high-quality math tutoring regardless of whether they live in Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, or elsewhere in Finland.

Tutoring is available in both Finnish and English.

Free introductory lesson

The first 30-minute introductory lesson is completely free and does not commit you to further lessons.

During the introductory lesson, the student’s current skills are assessed, goals are discussed, and a suitable approach for future lessons is planned.
